Shipping Policy

Shipping options and rates
We offer three shipping speeds at checkout: Standard (5-7 business days, $4.99, free on orders over $50), Expedited (2-3 business days, $12.99), and Overnight (1 business day, $24.99, order must be placed before 1pm local time). Rates and availability vary slightly by destination and are calculated at checkout based on package weight and delivery address.

Processing time
Orders are processed and packed within 1-2 business days of being placed, Monday through Friday, excluding public holidays. Orders placed after 3pm local time are processed the next business day. Processing time is separate from shipping transit time, so a Standard order placed on a Thursday would ship Friday or Monday and arrive 5-7 business days after that.

Order tracking
Once your order ships, you'll receive a confirmation email with a tracking number and carrier link. Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to update after the label is created. You can also view tracking status anytime from Order History in your account.

International shipping
We currently ship to over 40 countries. International orders may be subject to customs fees, import duties, and taxes charged by the destination country; these are the responsibility of the customer and are not included in the order total. International delivery times range from 7-21 business days depending on destination and customs processing.

Address changes
If you need to change a shipping address, contact support as soon as possible. We can update the address only if the order has not yet entered the packing stage. Once a tracking number has been generated, we're unable to redirect the package, and you'll need to coordinate directly with the carrier or contact us to arrange a return.

Lost or delayed packages
If tracking shows no movement for more than 5 business days (domestic) or 10 business days (international), contact support and we'll open an investigation with the carrier. If a package is confirmed lost in transit, we will send a free replacement or issue a full refund, customer's choice.

Delivery issues
If a package shows as "delivered" but you haven't received it, first check with neighbors and building management, as carriers sometimes mark packages delivered slightly before the customer receives them. If it hasn't turned up within 48 hours, contact support so we can file a claim with the carrier.

Split shipments
Orders containing multiple items may ship separately if items are fulfilled from different warehouses. You'll receive a separate tracking number for each shipment at no additional cost.
