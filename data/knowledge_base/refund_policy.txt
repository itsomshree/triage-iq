Refund Policy

We want you to be happy with every purchase. If you're not satisfied, here's how refunds work.

Eligibility
Items are eligible for a full refund if the return request is made within 30 days of the delivery date. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, and include all accessories and documentation that shipped with it. Items marked "final sale" at checkout are not eligible for refund, but may still qualify for store credit at our discretion.

How to request a refund
Log into your account, go to Order History, select the order, and click "Request Refund." You'll be asked for a reason for the return. Once submitted, you'll receive a prepaid return shipping label by email within 24 hours. Refunds are not processed until the returned item has been received and inspected by our warehouse team.

Processing time
Once your return is received, inspection typically takes 2-3 business days. After approval, refunds are issued to the original payment method and typically appear within 5-10 business days, depending on your bank or card issuer. We do not offer cash or check refunds.

Partial refunds
If an item is returned used, damaged, or missing parts not due to our error, we may issue a partial refund reflecting the reduced value of the item. Customers will be notified of the reduced amount before the refund is processed.

Refunds for digital products
Digital products (software licenses, downloadable content, gift cards) are non-refundable once the license key or download link has been delivered, except where required by law.

Late or missing refunds
If more than 10 business days have passed since your refund was approved and you have not received it, first check with your bank or card issuer, as processing times vary. If the refund still hasn't posted, contact support and reference your order number so we can investigate.

Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. If you'd like a different size, color, or model, please request a refund for the original item and place a new order.

Damaged or defective items
If an item arrives damaged or defective, contact support within 7 days of delivery with photos of the damage. Damaged or defective items are eligible for a full refund or free replacement, and we will cover return shipping costs in these cases.
